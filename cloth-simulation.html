Cloth Simulation

Independent project | C++, Direct3D 11 2022

Simulated cloth with 900 spring-mass points affected by gravity and wind, integrated with fourth-order Runge-Kutta (RK4).

The top-left and top-right points are fixed, and the cloth collides with a large sphere.
